moonbadge

Generator badge SVG zero-dependency berestetika lunar — 6 gaya, 9 ikon, offline & self-hosted. Setiap badge di halaman ini dirender oleh pustaka yang sama yang ada di repo.

Gaya & Warna

flat — gaya klasik, palet lunar bawaan

python3 -m moonbadge "agent" "aktif"

flat-square — siku tajam, warna kustom

python3 -m moonbadge "deps" "nol" --style "flat-square" --right-color "#10b981"

plastic — kilau glossy ala badge klasik

python3 -m moonbadge "pulse" "hidup" --style "plastic"

for-the-badge — besar, kapital, tebal

python3 -m moonbadge "lunarjjo" "otonom" --style "for-the-badge"

lunar — gradien violet + watermark bulan sabit

python3 -m moonbadge "agent" "v0.1.0" --style "lunar" --icon "moon"

lunar + ikon centang

python3 -m moonbadge "tests" "lulus" --style "lunar" --icon "check"

lunar + ikon kilau

python3 -m moonbadge "pulse" "24/7" --style "lunar" --icon "spark"

glass — pil gelap + bar aksen

python3 -m moonbadge "uptime" "99.9%" --style "glass" --icon "bolt"

glass tanpa ikon

python3 -m moonbadge "lisensi" "MIT" --style "glass"

flat + warna kustom + ikon terminal

python3 -m moonbadge "python" "3.8+" --icon "terminal" --right-color "#3776ab"

flat + ikon branch git

python3 -m moonbadge "ci" "github actions" --icon "branch" --right-color "#2088ff"

lunar + ikon bintang

python3 -m moonbadge "mood" "tenang" --style "lunar" --icon "star"
